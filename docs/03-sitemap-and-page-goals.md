# 03 — Sitemap & Page Goals

## Public sitemap

MTS is a net-new website (no legacy site/domain being migrated), so route names use a clean semantic structure rather than mirroring the reference site's URLs:

```text
/
├── /about
├── /products
│   └── /products/[slug]
├── /collections
│   └── /collections/[slug]
├── /services
│   └── /services/[slug]
├── /blog
│   └── /blog/[slug]
├── /faq
├── /contact
├── /search
├── /privacy-policy
└── /terms
```

If an existing indexed Mian Tayyab Steel website/domain is supplied later, perform a URL crawl and redirect assessment (see `12-seo-and-url-strategy.md`) before production launch.

## Homepage

### Goal
Establish credibility, communicate catalogue breadth, help visitors self-select products/applications and push qualified inquiries.

### Primary CTA
Request a Quote

### Secondary CTA
Explore Products / WhatsApp Sales

### Required content
- brand positioning
- key product families
- application discovery
- credibility/history
- distributor/certification proof
- services preview if strategically useful
- quote CTA

## About

### Goal
Turn history and operating credibility into trust.

### Content
- company introduction
- founder story
- generational timeline
- vision
- mission
- scale/reach only where factual
- company profile download
- locations/operations teaser

## Products archive

### Goal
Fast catalogue discovery.

### Content
- page intro
- collection/application shortcuts
- product grid
- search/filter only if it adds genuine value
- quote support CTA

## Product detail

### Goal
Answer technical and purchase-intent questions, then convert.

### Content order
1. breadcrumb
2. media + identity/summary
3. primary CTA
4. key specifications
5. overview
6. applications
7. features/benefits
8. grades/sizes/origin where relevant
9. related services
10. downloads
11. FAQ
12. related products
13. final inquiry CTA

## Collection/application detail

### Goal
Rank for high-intent category/application queries and guide users to suitable products.

### Content
- category proposition
- explanation/education
- applicable products
- solution groups
- use cases/applications
- industries
- selection guidance
- comparison data
- delivery/reach content where accurate
- FAQ
- quotation CTA

## Services archive

### Goal
Explain processing capabilities and route users to relevant service pages.

### Content
- service grid
- operational trust/certifications
- relationship to products
- inquiry CTA

## Service detail

### Goal
Explain capability, suitability and inquiry requirements.

### Content
- service media
- service summary
- capability details
- suitable materials/use cases
- accepted requirements/drawing guidance when known
- CTA
- FAQ
- related products/services

## Blog archive

### Goal
SEO + education + authority.

### Content
- featured/latest articles
- categories/tags only if enough content exists
- article cards with readable dates/excerpts

## Article detail

### Goal
Answer a search/query thoroughly and lead the reader naturally to relevant commercial pages.

### Content
- breadcrumb
- article title
- metadata
- feature image
- article body
- optional table of contents for long articles
- relevant products/services
- contextual quote CTA
- related articles

## FAQ

### Goal
Resolve common pre-sales friction and reinforce internal linking.

## Contact

### Goal
Make reaching the correct team effortless.

### Content
- contact channels
- locations
- quote/contact form
- map(s)
- office/warehouse labels
- opening hours only if verified

## Search

### Goal
Allow high-intent visitors to quickly find a product, service, category or article.
